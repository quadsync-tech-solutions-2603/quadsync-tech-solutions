Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later or upgrade mid-way?

Yes. You can upgrade from Basic to Standard or Advanced at any time — we'll simply adjust the difference and continue from where you left off, with no restart needed.

Is there a long-term contract or lock-in?

Monthly retainer services are billed month-to-month with no long-term lock-in. One-time services (like websites or SEO setup) are paid once for the scope delivered.

What if my requirement doesn't match any listed plan?

That's common — most businesses need a mix. Reach out on WhatsApp or email and we'll put together a custom package combining services and pricing that fits your exact needs.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. Plan pricing covers everything listed in the features. The only exceptions are third-party costs called out explicitly, such as ad spend for Paid Ads campaigns, which is billed directly by Google or Meta.

How soon can I get started?

Most engagements kick off within 24–48 hours of confirming your plan and requirements. Training program batches start on their announced batch dates.
